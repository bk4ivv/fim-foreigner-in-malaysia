# FIM Jobs & Community Plan

**Recorded:** 2026-11-09
**Status:** Planning only — no app build or redesign started in this session.

## Product direction

FIM will not recruit, employ, or act as an employment agency. The planned FIM Jobs feature is a connection marketplace: employers publish opportunities and job seekers contact employers directly. FIM should not promise visas, permits, job placement, salary, or employer changes.

Initial job categories:

- Temporary / contract work
- Part-time work
- Permanent / full-time work
- Employer-posted vacancies

Recommended first release:

- Guest browsing for public information
- Optional job-seeker account
- Optional employer account
- Employer job posting with company/contact details
- Search and filters for job type, location, salary, language, and availability
- Direct employer–job-seeker contact
- Report, block, moderation, and scam-warning controls
- Clear disclaimer that hiring, contracts, payment, work permits, and legal compliance remain the responsibility of the employer and worker

## Safety and compliance direction

FIM should begin as a listing and contact platform, not a recruitment agency. Before adding paid placement, candidate representation, contract handling, or foreign-worker recruitment services, confirm Malaysian legal and licensing requirements. Job listings must prohibit advance-payment scams, passport surrender, OTP/bank-password requests, illegal work, and guaranteed visa/permit claims.

## Account and security direction

Keep public services usable as guest. Require an optional account only for personal features such as saved jobs, applications/contact history, notifications, job-seeker profiles, employer posting, and synced settings. Use secure authentication, per-user access control, private document storage, account deletion, logout-all-devices, and minimal data collection. Never expose passport numbers, bank details, passwords, OTPs, or full private addresses in public profiles.
