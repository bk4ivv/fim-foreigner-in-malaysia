# Recording Comparison: v2.11.0 vs Later Broken Build

## Evidence from v2.11.0

The v2.11.0 recording shows the splash transition completing at approximately 00:05. The first country-selection screen is usable immediately, with the searchable list of 248 countries visible. After selecting Bangladesh and Bengali, the worker dashboard appears with official services and tools. The recording shows no persistent spinner during the country list, language selection, dashboard, learning, or credit navigation.

## Evidence from the later recording

The later recording shows the splash transition reaching the main screen at approximately 00:02, but the main content area is empty and a yellow central spinner remains visible through approximately 00:46. The footer remains usable: About/Privacy and Creator/Credit screens open and scroll normally, and the theme menu can switch between light and dark. Taps on the empty main content area do not reveal any content.

## Verified difference

The regression occurs after the splash screen and before the worker dashboard content is populated. It is therefore not primarily a native Android launch failure, an app-wide freeze, or a theme-navigation failure. v2.11.0 reaches country selection and then the dashboard; the later build reaches a main shell with footer/theme controls but remains in a content-loading state.

The exact failing boundary visible in the recordings is: **splash complete → main shell visible → dashboard/content future never resolves or never renders**. This comparison does not by itself identify whether the unresolved operation is country/language state restoration, a dashboard data future, or another initialization branch; that requires source/build-log inspection after the user authorizes the next repair step.

## Logo observation

The v2.11.0 recording uses the earlier Foreign Worker Malaysia branding. The newly supplied Malaysian flag image is a separate requested branding change and was not part of the v2.11.0 behavior comparison.
